# Layout Engine

## Layouts

### Meta ASC V1

- Structure: top headline, center product or subject, bottom offer or CTA.
- Best for: broad ecommerce, cold traffic, simple offers.
- Text: short headline, optional CTA.
- Visual hierarchy: product must be central and large.

### Social Proof Layout

- Structure: product plus review card, rating cue, customer quote.
- Best for: warm traffic, credibility building.
- Text: use only real or placeholder-labeled proof.
- Visual hierarchy: review supports product, not the reverse.

### Benefit Focus Layout

- Structure: product hero with 2-3 callouts.
- Best for: functional products, feature education.
- Text: bullets must be short and benefit-led.

### Transformation Split Layout

- Structure: before/after or problem/result split screen.
- Best for: visible transformation, organization, beauty, cleaning, home improvement.
- Text: label both sides clearly.
- Compliance: avoid unrealistic or medical transformation claims.

### Problem Solution Layout

- Structure: left/top problem cue, right/bottom product solution.
- Best for: products solving a recognizable daily issue.
- Text: problem phrase plus concise solution.

### Quote Review Card Layout

- Structure: native review card layered with product/lifestyle image.
- Best for: UGC and trust-building creatives.
- Text: keep quote short.

### Comparison Layout

- Structure: product compared with old alternative or generic behavior.
- Best for: competitive differentiation.
- Text: avoid naming competitors unless user provides permission.

### UGC Native Layout

- Structure: phone-shot style, imperfect but intentional framing.
- Best for: cold and warm audiences.
- Text: minimal overlay or social caption style.

### Product Callout Layout

- Structure: clean product hero with pointer callouts.
- Best for: technical features and value clarity.
- Text: 2-4 callouts max.

### Lifestyle Story Layout

- Structure: product embedded in a moment with light headline.
- Best for: emotional, gift, fashion, home, beauty.
- Text: aspirational but direct.
