- Studio product hero

- Gym / active routine
- Outdoor lifestyle

## Style Library

- High-conversion Facebook ad
- UGC native
- Minimal ecommerce
- Premium editorial

- Product plus callouts
- Infographic benefit
- Split-screen comparison
- Review card
- Soft feminine
- Bold masculine
- Warm family
- Fresh natural
- Western localized ad

## Selection Rules

- Match scene to product use, not just category.
- Use UGC style for trust and cold-audience testing.
- Use premium style for high price positioning.
- Use infographic/callout style for feature-heavy products.
- Use lifestyle style when the benefit is emotional or identity based.
- Avoid repeating the same scene-style pair in a batch.
