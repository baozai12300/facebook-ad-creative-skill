# Angle Engine

Use angles to prevent a batch from becoming one repeated ad with small visual changes.

## Core Angles

- Pain Solution: starts from a concrete frustration and presents the product as relief.
- Outcome Led: shows the desired end state first.
- Contrast: compares old vs new, messy vs organized, dull vs polished.
- Emotional Resonance: connects to confidence, ease, calm, delight, or belonging.
- Scene Immersion: makes the product feel native to a daily moment.
- Social Proof: uses reviews, ratings, community language, or UGC framing.
- Expert Backing: uses professional, technical, or ingredient logic without fake credentials.
- Gift: frames the product as an easy, thoughtful present.
- Offer: highlights discount, bundle, free shipping, or limited-time value.
- Minimal Aesthetic: premium visual focus with low text.
- Feature Benefit: turns product features into buyer benefits.
- Before After: visual transformation, only when truthful and compliant.
- Lifestyle: associates the product with identity and routine.
- Seasonal Trend: ties to a current season, holiday, or trend without forcing it.

## Batch Selection Rules

- 4 creatives: use at least 4 different angles.
- 6 creatives: include at least one proof/UGC angle and one product clarity angle.
- 8 creatives: include at least two cold-audience angles, two warm-audience angles, and one hot-audience offer angle.
