# Design Philosophy

This skill is built around structured creative exploration, not magic performance claims.

Meta ad systems need creative signals that are different enough to test. For ecommerce teams, the practical problem is that many AI-generated ad images look like slight variations of the same template. This skill solves that by separating creative generation into explicit dimensions:

- audience hypothesis
- buyer motivation
- pain point or desire
- selling angle
- scene
- style
- layout
- copy density
- color and camera language

## ASC-Friendly Thinking

For Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns and broad Meta delivery, the goal is to provide a varied creative pool that gives the system more distinct material to explore. The skill therefore favors:

- multiple audience assumptions
- multiple visual signals
- cold, warm, and hot traffic angles
- mobile-readable product value
- direct but not overloaded text
- clear product focus
- UGC and lifestyle variants alongside product clarity variants

The skill never claims that a creative will be approved, win auctions, improve ROAS, or beat the algorithm.

## Creative Modes

- Single Creative Mode: one polished ad concept.
- Batch Testing Mode: multiple highly distinct ad hypotheses.
- UGC Native Mode: social-feed-native, lower-polish trust creative.
- Studio Catalog Mode: product clarity and feature focus.
- Offer Promotion Mode: direct response with offer hierarchy.
- Problem-Solution Mode: recognizable pain point and product relief.
- Review / Social Proof Mode: credibility-led creative, only with real or clearly placeholder proof.

## Text Overlay Modes

- none: visual only.
- light: headline and optional CTA.
- standard: headline, subheadline, 1-3 bullets, CTA.
- heavy: stronger poster/informational layout, use sparingly.
